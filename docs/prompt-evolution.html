Fifteen rewrite prompts, before and after optimization

Each starting prompt was evolved by a meta-model that read per-judge rank improvements on training batches and proposed revisions. The best one on validation was kept. On average the prompts grew from 90 to 240 words, and most styles ended up close to the same recipe: a summary, keyword-rich sections, features linked to user needs, and answers to likely questions.
